Suresse Direkt Bank App

Manage your savings easily and securely from your smartphone with the Suresse Direct Bank mobile banking app, built natively in UIKit.

Secure login: Authenticate with your password or Face ID, and the app automatically locks after 5 minutes of inactivity for your protection. Account overview: Check your balance at a glance, wherever you are. Money transfers: Send funds quickly and effortlessly. Profile management: Update your personal details directly within the app. Account opening: Open a new savings account or fixed-term deposit in just a few taps. Interest calculator: Simulate interest earnings to plan your savings goals. Document downloads: Export your statements and annual report as PDFs for easy record-keeping.

Under the hood, the app follows a clean MVVM architecture with all business logic encapsulated in a Swift “use-case” layer. ViewModels expose Combine publishers that drive real-time UI updates and form validations, while dependency injection via protocols ensures components remain loosely coupled and fully mockable. This structure enables comprehensive unit tests of view models and use cases, snapshot tests for UIKit views, and end-to-end UI tests for critical flows such as login, transfers, and account opening.
