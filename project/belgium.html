Santander Consumer Bank

Belgium

Manage your savings safely and easily from your smartphone with the Santander Consumer Bank Mobile Banking app for Belgium. Santander Consumer Finance, S.A. – Belgian branch, is headquartered in Merelbeke-Melle and part of the global Banco Santander group.

Account management: Check your savings balance, view transaction history, and download account statements or your annual report.

Secure access: Log in with your username and password or enable Face ID; the app auto-logs out after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Transfers & payments: Execute SEPA transfers instantly or schedule them for later.

Personal profile: Update your personal details and manage multiple accounts.

New accounts: Open savings or fixed-term deposit accounts directly within the app.

Interest simulator: Calculate projected earnings to plan your savings goals.

Under the hood, this Belgian version mirrors the advanced architecture of its German counterpart:

Built natively with UIKit, it leverages a clean MVVM structure where view controllers bind to view models that expose Combine publishers for real-time UI updates and form validation.

All business logic resides in a Swift “use-case” layer, while dependency injection via protocol abstractions keeps modules loosely coupled and fully mockable.

Local caching (Core Data) and background prefetching optimize performance and enable offline browsing of your saved data.

A disciplined testing strategy includes unit tests for view models and use cases, snapshot tests for key screens, and end-to-end UI tests covering critical flows such as login, transfers, and account opening.

By combining UIKit’s maturity with Combine’s reactive streams and a robust MVVM core, the Santander Consumer Bank Belgium app delivers a seamless, secure, and highly responsive mobile banking experience—virtually identical in quality and functionality to its German sibling.
