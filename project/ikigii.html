ikigii

TowerBank Crypto App

Manage your cryptocurrencies and dollar account seamlessly in one native SwiftUI app, powered by Towerbank’s regulated infrastructure.

Crypto & Dollar Wallet: Receive, send and convert Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT and USDC—XRP support coming soon—and hold your funds in a dollar-denominated account.

Global Debit & Credit Cards: Order a card with free shipping, use it anywhere in the world, withdraw cash at any ATM, and set your own credit limits secured by stablecoins.

International Transfers & Payments: Send SEPA and SWIFT transfers, pay online or in-store, and get real-time push notifications for every movement.

Quick Onboarding: Open your Ikigii account in minutes from any country, make your first deposit in crypto or dollars, and you’re ready to go.

Enterprise-Grade Security: Built on a trust model—your cryptos are held in a Panamanian bank trust—and protected by biometric login, end-to-end encryption, and automatic session timeouts.

Personalized Support & Insights: Access 24/7 professional assistance and risk ratings, view detailed statements and annual reports, all within the app.

Under the hood, Ikigii is a showcase of modern SwiftUI development: a clean MVVM architecture with a Swift “use-case” layer encapsulating business rules, Combine publishers driving live balance and transaction streams, and dependency injection via protocols for fully mockable components. Network calls use URLSession + Combine pipelines with secure token management, while Core Data provides offline caching of your transaction history. Background tasks prefetch rates and card status for instant access. A comprehensive testing strategy—unit tests for view models and use cases, snapshot tests for SwiftUI views, and end-to-end UI tests—ensures reliability as the app evolves.
