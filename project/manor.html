Manor

Eccomerce App

Discover all the benefits of Manor, Switzerland’s largest department-store chain, right on your iPhone. Manor AG, headquartered in Basel, has been bringing style to life since 1902 and belongs to the Maus Frères Holding group.

Loyalty made digital: Scan your Manor card or add it to Wallet to collect and redeem points instantly, unlock exclusive offers, and view your tier status. Personalized deals: Get push notifications for tailored discounts and flash sales based on your shopping history.

Full catalog & ordering: Browse fashion, beauty, home, electronics, and gourmet foods; add items to cart and choose home delivery or in-store pickup without leaving the app. Store finder: Locate any of Manor’s 61+ stores across Switzerland, check opening hours, and get directions.

Secure account management: Update your profile, review order history, and download digital receipts on demand.

Under the hood, the app is a showcase of modern UIKit development:

MVVM architecture with a Swift “use-case” layer keeps UI code decoupled from business logic. Combine publishers deliver reactive updates for balances, offers, and cart state. Dependency Injection via protocol abstractions ensures components are loosely coupled and fully mockable.

Local caching with Core Data provides offline browsing of your cart and wish list. Background prefetching optimizes catalog loading and images. Comprehensive testing: Unit tests cover view models and use cases; snapshot tests validate key UIKit screens; and end-to-end UI tests verify critical flows like loyalty enrollment, checkout, and order tracking.

By combining UIKit’s flexibility with a robust MVVM/Combine core, Manor’s iOS app delivers a seamless, secure, and highly responsive shopping experience—bringing the department-store right into your pocket.
