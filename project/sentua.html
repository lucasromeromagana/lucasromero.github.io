Sentua

Eccomerce App

Discover and shop the finest cosmetics and perfumes on the go with Sentua, a fully native SwiftUI e-commerce app.

Curated catalog: Browse by category, brand or trending collections with beautifully animated product grids.

Product details: View high-resolution images, ingredient lists, customer reviews, and related recommendations.

Smart search & filters: Find exactly what you need using keyword search, price ranges, ratings, and scent profiles.

Seamless cart & checkout: Add items to your cart, apply promo codes, and complete purchases with Apple Pay or credit card—all in a few taps.

Order tracking: Monitor shipment status in real time and view past orders from your profile. Wishlist & favorites: Save products for later and receive alerts when they’re back in stock or on sale.

Push notifications: Get notified about flash sales, special offers, and restocks of your favorite items.

Under the hood, Sentua is built entirely in SwiftUI and employs a clean MVVM architecture. All business logic lives in Swift “use-case” modules, while view models expose Combine publishers that drive UI updates and manage form state. A standout feature is its advanced WebView integration: custom WKWebView coordinators bridge web-based product pages and promotional content seamlessly into SwiftUI, with JavaScript message handlers for dynamic content injection, smart caching layers for fast load times, and secure cookie/session management. Dependency injection via protocols keeps components loosely coupled and fully mockable—enabling comprehensive unit tests for view models and use cases, snapshot tests for SwiftUI views, and end-to-end UI tests of complex WebView flows.
